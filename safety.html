Child Safety & CSAE Standards

App Name: The Red Thread

Zero Tolerance Policy: The Red Thread has a zero-tolerance policy regarding the exploitation of children. We strictly prohibit Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and any content that endangers minors.

1. Standards and Prohibited Conduct

We strictly prohibit any user from uploading, sharing, or promoting content that involves the sexual exploitation or abuse of children. This includes, but is not limited to, the solicitation of minors or the distribution of prohibited imagery.

2. Content Moderation

To ensure a safe environment, we employ the following measures:

  • Manual Review: Our team monitors flagged content and user reports daily.
  • Automated Safeguards: We utilize industry-standard tools to identify and block prohibited content before it is shared.

3. Reporting Mechanisms

If you encounter any content or behavior that violates these standards, please take immediate action:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the "Report" button located on user profiles or message threads.
  • Email Support: Send an immediate report to threredthreadapp@gmail.com with the subject "URGENT: Safety Report".

4. Law Enforcement Cooperation

In accordance with international law, we report all instances of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate fully with global law enforcement agencies to ensure the safety of minors.
