The Red Thread

Privacy Policy & Support Center

1 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

Camera Access

The Red Thread requires access to your device's camera to allow you to capture "Snap Sparks." Photos are processed locally on your device to generate activity ideas and are only shared with your selected friends. We do not store your raw photos on our servers.

Location Data

We use your location to find nearby "Sparks" (activities, cafes, and events). This data is used only when the app is active and is not tracked in the background or sold to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with IMDB for movie data and uses affiliate links for Viator and Amazon. These services collect data according to their own privacy policies.
