Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play and Apple App Store policies, we provide a simple way for you to request the deletion of your account and associated data.

How to delete your data:

Please send an email to [Your Support Email Here] with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

In your email, please provide:

  • The email address or Username associated with your account.
  • A brief statement confirming you want your account and all associated data permanently removed.

Note: Once processed, this action is permanent. All your data, including progress and history, will be erased from our servers within [e.g., 7 days].
